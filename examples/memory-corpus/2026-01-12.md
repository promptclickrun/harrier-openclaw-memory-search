# Memory: 2026-01-12

## Project intake: ceramic kiln calibration

Took a call about a temperature-control calibration job for a small ceramics
studio. Their electric kiln is drifting during slow ramp schedules, which is
causing inconsistent glaze results across test tiles. They want a repeatable
process for validating thermocouple readings before each production run.

Decision: yes, but quote per calibration session, not per-hour, because each
kiln load and firing profile has different stabilization time. Standard
pipeline is baseline sensor check, three controlled ramp holds, controller log
export, and a short variance report. We do not change production recipes until
the studio signs off on the calibration report.

## Contact preferences

The facilities client prefers email over phone and wants a weekly status note,
not daily. They are sensitive about unnecessary process changes and explicitly
do not want automated recipe tuning. Keep the raw controller logs untouched and
deliver recommendations as a separate report.
